# 120 Best Pick Up Lines to Use IRL and in Text (For Every Mood)

Pick up lines that are funny, flirtatious, and perfect for impressing or just making your romantic interest laugh.

October 27, 2025

Grace Moser

![Text messages with pick up lines.](https://cdn.shortpixel.ai/spai/q_lossy+ret_img+to_auto/www.chasingfoxes.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/pick-up-lines-13.png)

Being able to flirt IRL or over text is kind of an art.

Because whether you wanna be more flirtatious and fun in your own relationship, grab the attention of someone you legit like, or even send a smooth message to a dating app match, it has to be done in **_just_** the right way.

You have to understand the mood and execute properly. **It takes precision**. 🤌

Pick up lines can have a tendency of being a bit cringy and corny, but I think that if it’s done just right, it’s probably gonna help (not hurt) **[your dating life](https://www.chasingfoxes.com/date-night-ideas/)** or relationship.

Also, beyond impressing someone with a perfectly smooth one-liner, a good pick up line can be a great way to break the ice, make someone laugh, or get a conversation started. 🤷‍♀️

And while finding new ways to flirt over text or in person can be kind of awkward, I’ve curated some of the best pick up lines to make it a bit easier.

You’re gonna find anything from funny pick up lines (with a dad joke feel) to romantic, cute, and even clever pick up lines. So whether you know the person would actually laugh at corny pick up lines or they’d probably like something a bit more sweet, I’ve got you covered. 👍

Also, if these pick up lines work, then here are some **[date night ideas](https://www.chasingfoxes.com/date-night-ideas/)** to take things to the next level.
